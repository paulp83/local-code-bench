# Club policy v2

### 1.1 Scope

This policy covers all club members.

### 1.2 Definitions

A member is anyone with a registered account.

### 2.1 Posting

Posts must be on topic and in English.

### 2.2 Links

Links must be to public pages.

### 2.3 Attachments

Attachments may not exceed 5 MB.

### 3.1 Moderation

Moderators may remove posts without notice.

### 3.2 Appeals

Appeals are answered within 7 days.

### 4.1 Data

We keep logs for 30 days.

### 4.2 Contact

Write to the admin address.

### 4.3 Retention

Accounts idle for 2 years are deleted.
